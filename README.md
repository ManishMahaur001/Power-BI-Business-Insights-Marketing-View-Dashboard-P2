# Power-BI-Business-Insights-Marketing-View-Dashboard-P2

Business Insights 360 -- Marketing View
📌 Project Overview
Business Insights 360 is a Power BI business intelligence project
designed to help stakeholders understand business performance through
interactive dashboards. This Marketing View focuses on marketing and
market performance, helping users explore sales contribution, customer
and product performance, and profitability-related metrics.
🎯 Objectives
Analyze marketing and market performance using business KPIs.
Compare performance across markets, customers, products, and time
periods.
Track key measures such as Net Sales, Gross Margin, Net Profit, and
Market Share.
Support data-driven decisions with interactive filters and visual
comparisons.
📊 Key Metrics
Net Sales -- overall sales after relevant deductions.
Gross Margin and Gross Margin % -- profitability after cost of
goods sold.
Net Profit and Net Profit % -- overall business profitability.
Market Share -- the business's share of the market.
Sales Quantity -- quantity sold across products and markets.
Forecast Accuracy -- comparison of forecasted and actual
quantities where available.
🔍 Business Analysis & Recommendations
Use the report to identify: - High-performing markets: Invest in
marketing and distribution where sales and profitability are strong. -
Low-performing markets: Investigate weak demand, pricing, product
availability, and campaign effectiveness before increasing spend. -
Customer and product opportunities: Prioritize valuable customer
segments and products that contribute strongly to sales and margin. -
Profitability gaps: Review discounts, deductions, and operating
costs when sales are growing but margins are declining. - Market-share
opportunities: Compare share and growth across markets to identify
areas where targeted campaigns may help. - Forecasting issues: Use
forecast accuracy and error measures to improve demand planning and
campaign coordination.
Recommendations should be validated against the actual filters, time
period, and values shown in the report. This README does not claim
specific numeric results.

🚀 How to Use
Download the `.pbit` file from this repository.
Open it in Power BI Desktop.
Follow the prompts to connect or configure the required data
sources, if requested.
Refresh the data and review the report filters and visuals.
Use slicers and comparisons to investigate performance by market,
customer, product, and time.
⚠️ Notes
This repository contains a Power BI template (`.pbit`), not a
standard `.pbix` report file.
Results depend on the connected dataset and the filters selected.
Confirm data-source access and refresh settings before using the
report for business decisions.
